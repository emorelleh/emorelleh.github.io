        <h1>BLUECRIME-GOV</h1>

        <h2>EU Blue Criminology and Ocean Governance</h2>

        <p>
          Since 1 October 2026, I have held the Jean Monnet Chair in
          EU Blue Criminology and Ocean Governance (BLUECRIME-GOV)
          at Universitat Jaume I.
        </p>

        <p>
          The Chair connects teaching, research, and public engagement
          on the European Union’s response to crime and environmental
          harm in the marine environment. It brings together criminology,
          European Union law, and the humanities through an Earth system
          perspective.
        </p>

        <p>
          The project runs from 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2029
          under Grant Agreement No. 101319975.
        </p>
      </section>
