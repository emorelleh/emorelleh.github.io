<p class="project-kicker">About BLUECRIME-GOV</p>
        <h2>Bringing blue criminology into European Union Studies</h2>
        <p class="project-lead">
BLUECRIME-GOV develops an academic and research programme on the European
Union’s response to crime and environmental harm in the marine environment.
It brings together Criminology, European Union Law and the Humanities through
the emerging framework of Earth System Law.
</p>
        <div class="project-two-columns">
<div>
<p>
The Chair addresses a structural gap in European Union Studies: the
criminological, penal and socio-legal dimensions of EU ocean governance
remain comparatively underdeveloped and insufficiently connected to
systemic ecological limits.
</p>
            <p>
BLUECRIME-GOV examines EU legal and governance responses to environmental
crime at sea, including the relationship between criminal enforcement,
marine ecosystem protection, compliance regimes, and the resilience of the
Earth system.
</p>
            <p>
The Chair is embedded in a strategic research line at Universitat Jaume I
focused on marine environmental crime and ecological harm. It builds on
teaching, research and knowledge-transfer initiatives in blue criminology,
ocean governance and environmental criminal law.
</p>
</div>

      <section id="teaching">
<p class="project-kicker">Teaching</p>
        <h2>European Union Studies and environmental crime</h2>
        <p class="project-lead">
BLUECRIME-GOV integrates EU-related content into university teaching and
develops interactive, interdisciplinary and practice-oriented learning
activities linking European integration, environmental criminal law,
criminology and marine governance.
</p>
        <div class="project-card-grid">
<article class="project-card">
<h3>European Union Studies</h3>
<p>
EU institutions, policies, legal frameworks and governance are placed at
the centre of the Chair’s teaching activities.
</p>
</article>
          <article class="project-card">
<h3>Environmental criminal law</h3>
<p>
Teaching examines EU criminal-law responses to environmental offences,
liability regimes, enforcement and sanctions in marine contexts.
</p>
</article>
          <article class="project-card">
<h3>Experiential learning</h3>
<p>
Interactive activities, including case-based work on EU environmental
litigation, support active learning and transversal skills.
</p>
</article>
          <article class="project-card">
<h3>Geospatial approaches</h3>
<p>
Interdisciplinary seminars connect EU environmental crime with spatial
data, remote sensing and the monitoring of illegal activities at sea.
</p>
</article>
</div>
</section>
      <section id="research">
<p class="project-kicker">Research</p>
        <h2>An interdisciplinary agenda for ocean governance</h2>
        <p class="project-lead">
The Chair connects EU Studies with emerging approaches to environmental crime,
marine governance, ecological harm and systemic environmental protection.
</p>
        <div class="project-card-grid">
<article class="project-card">
<h3>EU environmental criminal law</h3>
<p>
Analysis of EU criminal-law responses to environmental offences,
enforcement and compliance in the marine environment.
</p>
</article>
          <article class="project-card">
<h3>Blue criminology</h3>
<p>
Criminological analysis of environmental crime, ecological harm and
victimisation affecting seas, coasts and oceans.
</p>
</article>
          <article class="project-card">
<h3>Ocean governance</h3>
<p>
Study of EU marine-governance frameworks, policy instruments and their
relationship with global environmental challenges.
</p>
</article>
          <article class="project-card">
<h3>Earth System Law</h3>
<p>
An ecocentric perspective connecting law and governance with planetary
boundaries and Earth-system resilience.
</p>
</article>
          <article class="project-card">
<h3>Marine environmental evidence</h3>
<p>
Exploration of legal, ecological and technological evidence for detecting,
analysing and responding to environmental harm at sea.
</p>
</article>
          <article class="project-card">
<h3>EU environmental-crime reform</h3>
<p>
Critical analysis of the implementation and implications of Directive
(EU) 2024/1203 on the protection of the environment through criminal law.
</p>
</article>
</div>
</section>
