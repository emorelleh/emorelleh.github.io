    <h1>BLUECRIME-GOV</h1>
    <p class="project-intro">
      EU Blue Criminology and Ocean Governance is a Jean Monnet Chair focused on
      the European Union’s response to crime and environmental harm in the marine
      environment.
    </p>

  <main class="project-body">

    <section>
      <h2>About</h2>
      <p>
        BLUECRIME-GOV develops an academic and research programme on the European
        Union’s response to crime and environmental harm in the marine environment.
        It brings together Criminology, European Union Law and the Humanities through
        the emerging framework of Earth System Law.
      </p>
      <p>
        The Chair addresses a structural gap in European Union Studies: the
        criminological, penal and socio-legal dimensions of EU ocean governance
        remain comparatively underdeveloped and insufficiently connected to systemic
        ecological limits.
      </p>
      <p>
        The project runs from 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2029 under the Erasmus+
        Jean Monnet Actions, Grant Agreement No. 101319975.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Teaching</h2>
      <p>
        BLUECRIME-GOV integrates EU-related content into university teaching and
        develops interdisciplinary and practice-oriented learning activities linking
        European integration, environmental criminal law, criminology and marine
        governance.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Research</h2>
      <p>
        The Chair connects EU Studies with environmental crime, marine governance,
        ecological harm and systemic environmental protection.
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>EU environmental criminal law</li>
        <li>Blue criminology</li>
        <li>Ocean governance</li>
        <li>Earth System Law</li>
        <li>Marine environmental evidence</li>
        <li>EU environmental-crime reform</li>
      </ul>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Chair holder</h2>
      <p>
        Esteban Morelle-Hungría is Chair holder of the Jean Monnet Chair in EU Blue
        Criminology and Ocean Governance at Universitat Jaume I.
      </p>
      <p>
        The Chair is led from the intersection of environmental criminal law and
        criminology, with a focus on marine environmental protection, environmental
        crime, blue criminology and European Union governance.
