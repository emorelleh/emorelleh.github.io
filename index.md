## Bio {#bio}

I am an Associate Professor of Criminal Law and Criminology at Universitat Jaume I. My work is situated at the intersection of law, criminology, and ecology, with a particular focus on green and blue criminology, environmental criminal law, ecological governance, and marine environmental harm.

I coordinate the Blue Governance Laboratory (BlueGovLab) at Universitat Jaume I and co-coordinate the Research Centre for Criminal Law, Criminology and Intelligence. I also direct the IbizaPreservation Chair on Blue Crime, a collaborative initiative devoted to research, teaching, and knowledge exchange on marine environmental harm and ecological justice.

I hold a PhD in Law from the University of Alicante, with European Doctorate distinction. I am currently undertaking doctoral research in Marine Ecology at the University of the Balearic Islands. My interdisciplinary work combines legal and criminological analysis with socioecological research on marine ecosystems, biodiversity, climate change, pollution, and environmental justice.

## Research interests {#research-interests}

- Green criminology, blue criminology, and ecocriminology
- Environmental criminal law, ecocide, and ecological justice
- Marine environmental harm, underwater noise, and ocean governance
- Planetary boundaries, climate change, and socioecological harms
- Restorative ecological justice and environmental policy

You can read more about my [research]({{ ['/research/' | relative_url](https://portalcientific.uji.es/investigadores/1327563/detalle) }}).

## Teaching {#teaching}

I teach criminal law, criminology, and criminal policy at undergraduate and postgraduate level. My teaching has included courses in criminal law, criminal justice, criminology, environmental issues, external placements, and research supervision.

I supervise undergraduate and master's dissertations, particularly in criminal law, criminology, environmental crime, ecological harm, and environmental justice. I have also contributed to teaching innovation projects and to the development of learning materials in green criminology.

You can read more about my [teaching]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}).
