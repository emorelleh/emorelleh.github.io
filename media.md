    <section id="knowledge-transfer">
      <h2>Knowledge transfer</h2>

      <p>
        Since the beginning of my research career, I have considered that
        environmental law and sustainability should not remain limited to
        academic publications. My public-engagement work includes talks in
        schools, science-education activities, public events, professional
        training, and collaborations with local, regional, and national media.
      </p>

      <p>
        In 2018, I joined the Climate Change Interdisciplinary Laboratory
        (LINCC) at the University of the Balearic Islands, where I developed
        research on environmental harm, green criminology, and the relationship
        between crime and climate change.
      </p>

      <p>
        I have also contributed to public and secondary-school activities,
        environmental-law events, and initiatives focused on green criminology.
        Through collaboration with media outlets including
        <em>Diario de Ibiza</em>, <em>NouDiari</em>, radio, television,
        and digital platforms, I seek to make research on environmental
        challenges accessible to broader audiences.
      </p>
    </section>
