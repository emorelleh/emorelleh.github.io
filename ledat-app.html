LEDAT® / IDPAm Calculator

Universitat Jaume I · CrimiClima project
This app implements the LEDAT® methodology and returns the Material Environmental Damage Index (IDPAm) on a scale of 0–12. It is an orientative tool for assessing the material seriousness of environmental harm and does not determine by itself whether a case constitutes a criminal offence or an administrative infringement.
