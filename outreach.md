    <section id="outreach-approach">
      <h2>Outreach and knowledge exchange</h2>

      <p>
        My outreach work connects academic research with public institutions,
        environmental organisations, professionals, students, the media,
        and communities affected by social and ecological challenges.
      </p>

      <p>
        I understand knowledge exchange as a two-way process. Research can
        contribute evidence, concepts, and analytical tools to public debate,
        professional practice, and environmental policy. At the same time,
        the experience of institutions, practitioners, organisations, and
        local communities can identify problems that require further
        academic attention.
      </p>

      <p>
        My principal areas of public engagement include green and blue
        criminology, environmental criminal law, marine pollution,
        ecological justice, ocean governance, climate change,
        and the protection of marine and coastal ecosystems.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section id="initiatives">
      <h2>Public engagement initiatives</h2>

      <div class="outreach-item">
        <h3>Jean Monnet Chair for EU BlueCrime and Ocean Governance</h3>

        <p class="outreach-meta">
          Chair holder · Universitat Jaume I · From October 2026
        </p>

        <p>
          The Jean Monnet Action EU BlueCrime and Ocean Governance
          (EU BLUECRIME-GOV) supports teaching, research, and public engagement
          on European Union environmental criminal law, maritime governance,
          marine protection, and ocean-related environmental harm.
        </p>

        <p>
          The Chair is designed to connect academic work with legal and policy
          debates on environmental crime, coastal risk, marine ecosystems,
          and European approaches to ocean governance.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="outreach-item">
        <h3>
          <a href="https://ibizapreservation.org/en/ibizapreservation-and-the-universitat-jaume-i-de-castello-join-forces-to-promote-a-pioneering-classroom-on-blue-crime/">
            Aula IbizaPreservation de Criminalidad Azul
          </a>
        </h3>

        <p class="outreach-meta">
          Director · Universitat Jaume I and IbizaPreservation
        </p>

        <p>
          The Aula is a collaborative platform for research, education,
          professional training, and public engagement concerning crime,
          harm, and governance in marine environments.
        </p>

        <p>
          Its activities address issues such as illegal fishing, marine
          pollution, wildlife trafficking, underwater noise, pressures on
          <em>Posidonia oceanica</em>, environmental compliance, and the
          effects of human activity on aquatic ecosystems.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="outreach-item">
        <h3>
          <a href="https://bluegovlab.uji.es/">
            Blue Governance Laboratory (BlueGovLab)
          </a>
        </h3>

        <p class="outreach-meta">
          Scientific Coordinator · Universitat Jaume I
        </p>

        <p>
          BlueGovLab supports interdisciplinary collaboration on ocean
          governance, marine biodiversity, environmental protection,
          maritime security, and public policy.
        </p>

        <p>
          Its knowledge-exchange activities connect researchers from
          different disciplines with public authorities, civil-society
          organisations, environmental professionals, and actors working
          in the blue economy.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="outreach-item">
        <h3>
          <a href="https://clinilex.uji.es/">
            Legal and Criminological Clinic
          </a>
        </h3>

        <p class="outreach-meta">
          Former Director · Universitat Jaume I · 2023–2026
        </p>

        <p>
          From 2023 to September 2026, I directed the Legal and
          Criminological Clinic at Universitat Jaume I. The Clinic linked
          university teaching and research with real legal, criminological,
          and social needs through supervised projects developed in
          cooperation with public institutions, professionals, foundations,
          and civil-society organisations.
        </p>

        <p>
          Its activities promoted practical learning, professional
          responsibility, access to knowledge, environmental justice,
          and engagement with communities and groups facing situations
          of vulnerability.
        </p>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section id="societal-collaboration">
      <h2>Working with society</h2>

      <p>
        My knowledge-exchange activities involve collaboration with
        universities, public administrations, environmental foundations,
        civil-society organisations, legal and criminological professionals,
        security practitioners, and marine-conservation specialists.
      </p>

      <p>
        These collaborations include professional training, public seminars,
        technical reports, applied research, educational activities,
        communication of scientific findings, and the development of
        evidence-informed recommendations for institutions and organisations.
      </p>

      <p>
        I am particularly interested in collaborations that connect legal
        and criminological analysis with ecological knowledge and local
        experience, especially where they can contribute to preventing
        environmental harm and improving marine governance.
      </p>
    </section>
